# Self-care routines: content for clinician review

Status: **not reviewed**. Phase 29 shipped this wording as a draft. A clinician
should read every line below before the first public release, and the
answer goes in the table at the end. The text lives in
`lib/core/l10n/arb/app_en.arb`, so edits are made there and this file is
regenerated from it.

What the reviewer is asked to check:

- Nothing reads as treatment advice or a promise of relief.
- The stretches are safe for a general adult audience, and the stop line is
  clear enough.
- The paced breathing timing (4 seconds in, 6 out, six rounds) is suitable.
- The caffeine and sleep advice matches current headache guidance.
