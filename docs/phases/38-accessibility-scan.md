# Accessibility scan

Checks on every screen, at every scroll position, on a
360 × 720 phone:

- Text contrast (WCAG 1.4.3, 4.5:1 or 3:1 for large text)
- Every control has a label (WCAG 4.1.2)
- Tap targets at least 48 × 48 dp (Android)
- Tap targets at least 44 × 44 pt (iOS)
